# Constitution

## Project

- Name: Airbnb Seoul
- In one sentence: A website for searching stays and viewing reservations in Seoul

## Audience

- Main users: Travelers looking for accommodations in Seoul
- What they want to do: Search for stays, view reservations, browse inspiration for future getaways

## Principles

1. Search is the main action — the hero section centers on finding stays (from: "Start Your Trip with Airbnb" + search bar)
2. Reservations are always visible — users see their bookings at a glance (from: "Your reservations" section)
3. Inspiration helps future planning — browse destinations without searching (from: "Inspiration for future getaways" section)
